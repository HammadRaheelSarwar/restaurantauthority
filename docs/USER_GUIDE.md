# User Guide & Operations Manual
## Restaurant Electrical Authority (REA)

---

## 1. Getting Started & Logging In

Navigate to the REA application URL. On the login screen, enter your registered credentials or select your designated role profile.

### The 7 Role Experiences
1. **Super Admin**: Access the Global Platform Dashboard covering total organizations, system-wide compliance trends, and administrative settings.
2. **Organization Admin**: Manage your company's restaurant brands, physical locations, user invitations, and compliance audits.
3. **Restaurant Operator**: View multi-location scorecards, aggregate equipment maintenance logs, and high-priority corrective actions.
4. **Restaurant Manager**: Manage your designated location's daily electrical safety, report incidents, and participate in the manager certification academy.
5. **Electrical Assessor**: View your inspection schedule, execute on-site assessment checklists, attach photos, and issue findings.
6. **Certification Manager**: Review incoming manager certification applications, manage course syllabus, grade examinations, and approve certificates.
7. **Viewer**: Review compliance scores and historical audit records with read-only access.

---

## 2. Navigating Core Modules

### 2.1 Managing Restaurants & Locations
- From the sidebar, click **Restaurants** or **Locations**.
- Review the **Readiness Status** pill:
  - 🟢 **Compliant** (Score >= 85%): All systems operational and within safety thresholds.
  - 🟡 **Needs Attention** (Score 70-84%): Minor deficiencies or pending non-critical maintenance.
  - 🔴 **Non-Compliant** (Score < 70%): Critical electrical hazard detected or overdue corrective actions.

### 2.2 Site Assessments
- Assessors click **Assessments** > **Conduct Assessment**.
- Complete each checklist item:
  - Select **Pass**, **Fail**, **Needs Attention**, or **N/A**.
  - Enter specific observations.
  - Attach photographic evidence if required.
  - System automatically calculates the score and registers necessary corrective actions.
- Click **Submit Assessment** to send for final approval and generate the downloadable PDF report.

### 2.3 Equipment & Electrical Panels
- Track heavy food-service machinery and electrical distribution panels.
- Log technical specifications: amperage rating, voltage, 3-phase/single-phase, disconnect locations, and inspection schedules.

### 2.4 Incident Reporting & Corrective Actions
- If an electrical anomaly occurs (breaker trip, burning smell, loose wiring, water near outlet):
  - Click **Report Incident**.
  - Specify severity and equipment involved.
  - The system dispatches immediate notifications and registers an actionable corrective action with due date.

### 2.5 Manager Certification Academy
- Managers click **Certification Portal**.
- Submit an application and proceed through the 4 interactive training modules.
- Upon completing course content, take the online examination (timer active, multiple-choice & true/false questions).
- Scoring 80% or higher issues an official digital credential complete with verification QR code.
